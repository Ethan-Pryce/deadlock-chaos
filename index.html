The Rules

  1. Queue all three heroes. You must play (queue) these three heroes.
  2. Rolled items come first in their tier. You must buy each of your rolled items before any other (non-rolled) item of the same tier. A set's items may have different tiers and costs — that's fine, the rule is per tier.
  3. Max your rolled ability first. Only level your other abilities to below the level of your selected Max first ability.
  4. Complete the side quest(s). Tick each one off as you finish it.
  5. Chase a high score. Your score is +1 for every hero you leave selected in the hero pool, +3 for every mandatory item you buy, +10 for every side quest you complete and −5 for each side quest you fail. Side quests still unticked when you submit count as failed. Post your run to the leaderboard after the match.
